Open-source driver for Polono label printers

This installs a driver that lets Polono PL60, PL70e-BT and PL420 label printers print on any Mac running macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel. It doesn't need Rosetta or Polono's own drivers.

For each printer, it sends exactly what Polono's own driver sends. It has been tested on a real PL60, but not yet on a PL70e-BT or PL420.

If your printer is already set up and stopped printing (often with a "Filter failed" message), the installer switches it to this driver for you. Your printer keeps its name and settings.

If you haven't set up the printer yet, plug it in after installing, then add it in System Settings → Printers & Scanners.

This driver is free and is not made, endorsed or supported by Polono.
